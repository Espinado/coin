AI INFRASTRUCTURE INVESTMENT

CudaFlops lets you allocate capital into investment plans linked to AI infrastructure economics — with clear plan parameters and a transparent dashboard.

What this investment is
You buy a fixed-term investment plan. Each plan states a minimum amount, APR, and term. Profit accrues daily to your available balance. At maturity, principal returns to available balance and can be withdrawn to your own wallet.

How it works
1. Create an account and verify your email.
2. Top up with USDT or BTC (BTC converts to USDT at the platform rate where configured).
3. Choose a plan and open a contract.
4. Track daily accruals in the dashboard.
5. Withdraw available funds to your external wallet when ready.

Business model in plain terms
CudaFlops is built around investment-plan revenue and AI infrastructure economics — not around selling on-demand GPU hours or operating a public compute marketplace.

What this is not
CudaFlops is not a crypto exchange, not a bank, and not a GPU cloud provider for renting servers by the hour. Returns are not guaranteed.

Risks
Investing involves risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Please read the Risk Disclosure (/legal/risks) before participating.

Plan APR figures and landing calculators are illustrative. Live parameters are shown in the authenticated dashboard.
